Evidence-Centric Architecture (ECA)

No decision without evidence. No evidence without provenance. Evidence is immutable.

1. Micro-Observations

Lightweight client observers (DOM visibility, micro-pause cadence, scroll velocity, focus transitions) passively stream interaction events without collecting invasive personal biometrics.

2. Immutable Evidence

Raw observation streams are normalized, cryptographically hashed, and packaged into an append-only evidence graph referencing the session provenance.

3. Signed Proof of Attention

Verification engines evaluate evidence against policy rules and ML calibration models, issuing an Ed25519-signed Proof of Attention token certifying confidence.
